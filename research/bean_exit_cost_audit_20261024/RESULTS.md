# Exit-cost reserve audit

This is a same-forecast accounting comparison on already inspected development history. There are no new neural updates. The cached gross-mark policy must reproduce the original final equity exactly within 1e-6 before the comparison is accepted.

The old scenario value paid current orders but valued remaining inventory gross. The new value subtracts the expected closing fee from held inventory at both the current and future mark. This counts the round trip for a new position while avoiding repeated exit charges for an unchanged position. Both actual simulations still charge the same observed orders and final liquidation fees.

Accounting correctness and financial advantage are separate. All forecasts were generated by the previously trained Core model; no future outcomes enter the cached policy. A positive segment is not a new unseen evaluation or proof of beating other traders. Full decisions and weight-source hashes remain archived.
