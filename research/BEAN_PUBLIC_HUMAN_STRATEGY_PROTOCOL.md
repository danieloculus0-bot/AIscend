# Preregistered public-market BEAN study: learn from actual crypto fortunes and systematic edges

Status: **research protocol frozen before any new historical outcome run**. No live orders, trading permissions, wallet connection, capital transfer or auto-promotion. This branch is stacked on AIscend PR #115 and does not alter BEAN Core or funded app entrypoints.

## Motivating evidence, with limitations

The wealth stories matter, but they describe several different economically reproducible mechanisms rather than one magic trading indicator.

1. **Long exposure to an emerging asset, held across major volatility**: Winklevoss Capital's own historical timeline says it began purchasing BTC in 2012 and accumulated an estimated 1% of circulating supply. This is concentrated early-stage exposure, not evidence of repeated high-frequency timing skill. Source: https://www.winklevosscapital.com/about
2. **Early-cycle conviction and long holding**: Reported accounts of Dan Morehead/Pantera purchasing BTC at $65 in 2013 and holding through large drawdowns. This cannot be replicated by selecting coins with the benefit of future hindsight. Source: https://fortune.com/crypto/2025/08/04/dan-morehead-pantera-bitcoin-crypto-novogratz-briger-princeton/
3. **Risk-on momentum versus defensive cash**: Coinbase Institutional's June 26, 2026 study described a pre-set long/short BTC overlay with a 12-month momentum and 200-day trend regime. Its published backtest outperformed BTC holding but used short exposure and retrospective data. Our *long/cash* variant deliberately does not claim to reproduce the same returns. Source: https://www.coinbase.com/institutional/research-insights/research/weekly-market-commentary/weekly-2026-06-26
4. **Structural market-making / arbitrage**, not equivalent to momentum: A 2026 IEEE ICBC study describes >430,000 real spot trades on eight exchanges by a deployed low-latency arbitrage engine, with average reported net monthly profit around 2%. That advantage requires live venue integration, latency and capital, and is explicitly out of scope for this paper-only study. Source: https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/11575495
5. **Futures basis and carry**: A peer-reviewed 2026 study finds exceptionally high but variable crypto futures carry associated with trend-chasing demand and frictions. This is not an allowable spot-only action in this experiment and is not a promise of free returns. Source: https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.2024.05069

A wealthy survivor is not a sample of every entrant. Do not infer a repeatable method from a successful biography, or use the richest realized assets as an ex-post universe. The known BTC/ETH pair is fixed **before** fetching this run's market path. Selection bias from previous public research persists.

## Frozen protocol

- **Assets**: BTC-USD and ETH-USD, spot, public Coinbase Exchange native six-hour OHLCV. No private Coinbase APIs or authentication.
- **Data times**: [2024-01-01 00:00 UTC, 2026-11-01 00:00 UTC); both endpoints fixed and after the latest complete candle. Reject missing or duplicate bars and gaps. Do not forward-fill. Preserve exact raw public candle source SHA256, independent chunk receipt hashes, and UTC request boundaries.
- **Causal training**: The existing BEAN rotation shadow learner in PR #115, pinned to actual separate BEAN Core d13ea68fd2d0fd4cd7af1cd297e1fbec75a954fe. Initial 70% of the chronological bars development/training; final 30% retrospective evaluation. Only outcomes fully matured within training enter gradient updates. Two seeds 7 and 41, four passes through same training history, identical optimizer and example budgets.
- **Inputs**: Existing short-horizon momentum, volatility and position signals *plus historical-only* 200-day moving-average gap and 365-day momentum for each asset. Missing long lookback data is marked explicitly unavailable, not interpolated. These same values are visible to the model only after their historical bars close. The model is not told what it must trade.
- **Actions**: Cash, top trailing-16-bar return asset, runner-up; these can result in hold, defensively sell, rotate, reenter, or abstain. Cash means USD hypothetical balance, **not a real USDC stablecoin transaction**.
- **Execution**: Decision after close of bar t, fictional fill at open of t+2, outcome after close of t+8; 95 basis points of fee+spread per side, plus a 1%-of-candle-volume participation cap. Unfilled transitions retain existing inventory. Ending holdings are valued conservatively net of exit costs.
- **Predeclared controls**: cash; fixed BTC buy-and-hold; fixed strongest-momentum target; short-term trend-to-cash; **12-month-momentum plus 200-day-trend BTC long/cash**. The last rule buys/holds BTC unless both its trailing 12-month close change and its close relative to historical 200-day average are negative. This rule is a *long/cash translation* of Coinbase's published long/short overlay, not an equivalent return reproduction.
- **Matched outcome report**: Per seed returns net of fees, max drawdown, turnover/fees via full receipts, unfilled counts, prediction count, event SHA, bootstrap interval explicitly labeled a retrospective diagnostic; same-window controls and an unchanged untrained network. Recover and replay complete Core weights/optimizer/replay/RNG. Retain losses and failed cases, do not cherry-pick winner seeds.
- **No evidence inflation**: This is previously occurred public-market history, not genuinely prospective unseen-after-freeze performance. We will call it **retrospective public-market evaluation**, even if the policy wins in this single lookback. There is no external retrieved memory in this study, so a shuffled-memory placebo is N/A, not passed.
- **Future gate**: After the frozen code/data protocol and this historical diagnostic are reviewed, future completed bars can be appended in a *new* immutable prospective paper ledger without retraining on those bars, resetting the old hosted ledger or changing existing frozen strategy identities. Fee-net advantage against cash, BTC hold, fixed trend and untrained controls must persist on genuinely new time before any promotion discussion.
